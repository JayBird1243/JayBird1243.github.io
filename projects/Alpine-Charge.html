Alpine Charge

Co-founded a BC startup that keeps hikers powered at the trailhead; we provide autonomous charging kiosks, municipal partnerships, and industrial IoT remote management.

In March 2026, I co-founded Alpine Charge (Alpine Charging Solutions Inc.), a BC-based startup dedicated to keeping hikers safe and connected. We provide portable phone chargers via autonomous kiosks installed directly at popular trailheads (see gallery for photos).

Managing this venture has involved overseeing the entire product lifecycle; from negotiating hardware procurement with international suppliers to drafting and securing a municipal contract with the Village of Lions Bay. We coordinated directly with local public works and electrical departments to ensure seamless installation. The kiosks are powered by industrial IoT networking for remote management, featuring in-house designed branding and signage.

We are currently in the active operations phase, continuously monitoring and optimizing the project's performance.
