Alpine Charge

I co-founded Alpine Charging Solutions Inc. in March 2026. We run self-serve kiosks at trailheads that rent portable phone chargers to hikers, under a municipal contract with the Village of Lions Bay, BC.

  • We drafted and secured a contract with the Village of Lions Bay to put kiosks at the trailhead.
  • We sourced the kiosks from international suppliers and installed them with the village's public works and electrical departments.
  • The kiosks are managed remotely over an industrial IoT network and are operating today.
01

At the trailhead

A kiosk installed at the trailhead: screen on top, three bays of power banks, card terminal on the side.

Hikers rent a power bank at the start of the trail and return it to the kiosk afterwards. The kiosk runs unattended.

  1. Pay at the card terminal on the side of the kiosk.
  2. The kiosk unlocks a power bank from one of its bays.
  3. Take it on the trail. Lightning, USB-C and Micro USB cables are built in.
  4. Return it to any empty slot and push it in until it locks.

We designed the branding in-house: the logo, the screen artwork, the signage and the label on each power bank.
